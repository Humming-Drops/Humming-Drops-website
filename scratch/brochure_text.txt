ABOUT US
Humming Drops was founded on the simple
belief that achieving optimal health shouldn't be
a struggle. We are dedicated to delivering
essential daily nutrition right to your doorstep,
making wellness effortless and enjoyable.
Our Mission 
The Problem We are Solving
In today's fast-paced world, finding the time to
consistently add the right nutrients and vitamins
to your diet is a common challenge. Many
people miss out on the vital goodness of fresh
fruits and vegetables, impacting both their
physical and mental energy.
Our Solution 
Humming Drops solves this by offering a
convenient, monthly subscription service for fresh
mixed cut fruit boxes, delivered every morning.
We ensure you receive the natural vitamins and
nourishment you need through meticulously
prepared, high-quality produce.
More Than Just Fruits 
Your commitment to a healthy body with Humming
Drops also unlocks access to the MedCity Smiles
Community, in collaboration with MedCity Labs,
creating a holistic path to both physical and
mental happiness.
